## 📌 Overview & Mission

Because academic materials, internal assessment question papers, and lab manuals are often scattered or unavailable online, **JyothiFiles** was founded as an independent, student-led open education repository. 

Our mission is to empower every engineering student at **Jyothy Institute of Technology (Bengaluru)** with 24/7 instant access to:
- 📝 **Internal Assessment Question Papers** (CIE 1, CIE 2, CIE 3 / Pre-VTU)
- 🎓 **Semester End Exam (SEE) Question Papers**
- 🔬 **Updated Departmental Lab Manuals**
- 📐 **CAED (Computer-Aided Engineering Drawing) Solutions & Guides**
- 🎯 **Campus Placement Training & Aptitude Materials**

## 🤝 How to Contribute

Have a question paper, lab record, or subject handwritten notes that isn't listed here? Help your fellow juniors and batchmates!

1. **Email Your PDFs:** Send scanned PDFs or clear photos to **[hariprajwal704@gmail.com](mailto:hariprajwal704@gmail.com)** with the Subject line `[JIT Papers Contribution] - <Semester> - <Subject>`.
2. **Via Telegram:** Drop documents directly in the **[@Bteck2022](https://t.me/Bteck2022)** discussion group.
3. **Pull Request:** Fork this repository, add documentation/links, and create a Pull Request.
